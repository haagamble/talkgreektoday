## 1. Rooms

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">το δωμάτιο</td><td>room</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το σαλόνι</td><td>living room</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η κουζίνα</td><td>kitchen</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το υπνοδωμάτιο</td><td>bedroom</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το μπάνιο</td><td>bathroom</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

**Σε** combines with the definite article to show where something is:

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Article</th><th scope="col">Combined form</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">τον</td><td lang="el">στον</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">την</td><td lang="el">στην</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το</td><td lang="el">στο</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η μητέρα μου είναι <strong>στην κουζίνα</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">My mother is <strong>in the kitchen</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Το <strong>υπνοδωμάτιο</strong> είναι μικρό.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The <strong>bedroom</strong> is small.</p>
</div>

## 2. Furniture and things at home

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th><th scope="col">Plural</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">το τραπέζι</td><td>table</td><td lang="el">τα τραπέζια</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η καρέκλα</td><td>chair</td><td lang="el">οι καρέκλες</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το κρεβάτι</td><td>bed</td><td lang="el">τα κρεβάτια</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">ο καναπές</td><td>sofa</td><td lang="el">οι καναπέδες</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η ντουλάπα</td><td>wardrobe / closet</td><td lang="el">οι ντουλάπες</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η λάμπα</td><td>lamp</td><td lang="el">οι λάμπες</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το παράθυρο</td><td>window</td><td lang="el">τα παράθυρα</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

**Ο καναπές** has the plural **οι καναπέδες**. Its accusative forms are **τον καναπέ**, **τους καναπέδες**.

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Έχουμε ένα μεγάλο <strong>τραπέζι</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">We have a big <strong>table</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Οι <strong>καρέκλες</strong> είναι καινούριες.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The <strong>chairs</strong> are new.</p>
</div>

## 4. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek.

1. The table is in the kitchen.
2. We have four chairs.
3. My brother is in the living room.
4. Where is the bed?
5. The windows are big.
6. The bag is under the table.
7. The pencils are on the table.
8. Maria has a new sofa.
9. The lamp is not in the bedroom.
10. The wardrobe is small.
