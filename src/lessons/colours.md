## 1. Colours that keep one form

These colour words do not change form with the noun.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">μπλε</td><td>blue</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">πορτοκαλί</td><td>orange</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">ροζ</td><td>pink</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">μωβ</td><td>purple</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">γκρι</td><td>grey</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">καφέ</td><td>brown</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

The form stays the same with masculine, feminine, and neuter nouns, in the singular and plural, and in the nominative and accusative.

## 2. Examples with these colours

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η τσάντα μου είναι <strong>μπλε</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">My bag is <strong>blue</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Πού</strong> είναι το <strong>γκρι</strong> αυτοκίνητο;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>Where</strong> is the <strong>grey</strong> car?</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Δεν θέλω</strong> το <strong>πορτοκαλί</strong> βιβλίο.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I <strong>don’t want</strong> the <strong>orange</strong> book.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η Μαρία έχει δύο <strong>μωβ</strong> καρέκλες.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Maria has two <strong>purple</strong> chairs.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Το <strong>καφέ</strong> τραπέζι είναι <strong>στην τραπεζαρία</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The <strong>brown</strong> table is <strong>in the dining room</strong>.</p>
</div>

## 3. Colours that change

These colours change to match the gender of the noun.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">English</th><th scope="col">Masculine</th><th scope="col">Feminine</th><th scope="col">Neuter</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>red</td><td lang="el">κόκκινος</td><td lang="el">κόκκινη</td><td lang="el">κόκκινο</td></tr>
<tr><td>green</td><td lang="el">πράσινος</td><td lang="el">πράσινη</td><td lang="el">πράσινο</td></tr>
<tr><td>yellow</td><td lang="el">κίτρινος</td><td lang="el">κίτρινη</td><td lang="el">κίτρινο</td></tr>
<tr><td>black</td><td lang="el">μαύρος</td><td lang="el">μαύρη</td><td lang="el">μαύρο</td></tr>
<tr><td>white</td><td lang="el">άσπρος</td><td lang="el">άσπρη</td><td lang="el">άσπρο</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

The endings follow the same pattern for all five colours. Use **κόκκινος** as a model:

<tr><td>feminine singular</td><td lang="el">κόκκινη</td><td lang="el">κόκκινη</td></tr>
<tr><td>neuter singular</td><td lang="el">κόκκινο</td><td lang="el">κόκκινο</td></tr>
<tr><td>masculine plural</td><td lang="el">κόκκινοι</td><td lang="el">κόκκινους</td></tr>

</tbody></table></div>

## 4. Examples with changing colours

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Ο σκύλος μου είναι <strong>μαύρος</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">My dog is <strong>black</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η μητέρα μου έχει ένα μικρό, <strong>κόκκινο</strong> αυτοκίνητο.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">My mother has a small, <strong>red</strong> car.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Το <strong>άσπρο</strong> σπίτι έχει δύο μεγάλες <strong>άσπρες</strong> πόρτες.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The <strong>white</strong> house has two big white doors.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Βλέπω το <strong>κίτρινο</strong> σπίτι.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I see the <strong>yellow</strong> house.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Οι <strong>άσπρες</strong> πατάτες είναι στην κουζίνα.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The <strong>white</strong> potatoes are in the kitchen.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η <strong>κόκκινη</strong> ντομάτα μου είναι μεγάλη.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">My <strong>red</strong> tomato is big.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Έχω τρεις <strong>πράσινες</strong> ντομάτες.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I have three <strong>green</strong> tomatoes.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Τα <strong>κίτρινα</strong> μήλα είναι στο τραπέζι.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The <strong>yellow</strong> apples are on the table.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Δεν θέλω</strong> το <strong>μαύρο</strong> τραπέζι.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I <strong>don’t want</strong> the <strong>black</strong> table.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Πού</strong> είναι οι <strong>μαύρες</strong> καρέκλες;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>Where</strong> are the <strong>black</strong> chairs?</p>
</div>

## 5. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek.

1. I have a blue bag.
2. I see the grey car.
3. I want an orange book.
4. Maria has a pink car.
5. The brown table is in the dining room.
6. The red car is here.
7. I have a black car.
8. The door is green.
9. I see the yellow house.
10. The chairs are white.
