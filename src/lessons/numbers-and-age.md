## 2. Combining tens and units

Write the tens and units as separate words, without **και**:

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Number</th><th scope="col">Greek</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>25</td><td lang="el">είκοσι πέντε</td></tr>
<tr><td>37</td><td lang="el">τριάντα επτά</td></tr>
<tr><td>68</td><td lang="el">εξήντα οκτώ</td></tr>
<tr><td>99</td><td lang="el">ενενήντα εννέα</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

The final unit agrees with the noun’s gender: **είκοσι ένας σκύλοι**, **είκοσι μία γάτες**, **είκοσι ένα βιβλία**; **τριάντα τρεις γάτες**, **τριάντα τρία βιβλία**. For a masculine direct object, use **είκοσι έναν σκύλους**.

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Έχουμε <strong>σαράντα δύο βιβλία</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">We have <strong>forty-two books</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Έχω <strong>πενήντα επτά ευρώ</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I have <strong>fifty-seven euros</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η Μαρία έχει <strong>τριάντα έξι ευρώ</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Maria has <strong>thirty-six euros</strong>.</p>
</div>

## 3. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek. Write numbers in words.

1. Twenty-eight books.
2. We have thirty-four apples.
3. I have seventy euros.
4. Maria has sixty-three euros.
5. Twenty-one dogs.
6. We have seventy-four books.
7. Forty-nine houses.
8. I have eighty-six apples.
9. Thirty-three children.
10. One hundred houses.
