## 4. New words

- **ελληνικά** — Greek
- **η ταινία** — film
- **το μήνυμα** — message
- **η μουσική** — music
- **σήμερα** — today

## 5. Example sentences

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Μιλάω ελληνικά.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I speak Greek.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η Μαρία βλέπει την ταινία.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Maria is watching the film.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Γράφουμε ένα μήνυμα.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">We are writing a message.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Ο πατέρας μου δουλεύει.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">My father works.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Παίρνω το βιβλίο.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I take the book.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Ξεκινάς σήμερα;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Are you starting today?</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Ακούτε μουσική;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Do you listen to music? (Plural or polite.)</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Βλέπουν την ταινία;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Are they watching the film?</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Δεν φεύγω σήμερα.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I’m not leaving today.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Σταματάς;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Are you stopping?</p>
</div>

## 6. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek.

1. I speak Greek.
2. She watches the film.
3. We write a message.
4. They work here.
5. I take the book.
6. Do you listen to music? (One person, informal.)
7. He leaves today.
8. We are not stopping today.
9. They are watching the film.
10. Are you starting today?
