## 1. Asking what time it is

<tbody>

<tr><td lang="el">Ξέρεις τι ώρα είναι;</td><td>Do you know what time it is? (informal singular)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Ξέρετε τι ώρα είναι;</td><td>Do you know what time it is? (polite or plural)</td></tr>

## 2. Saying the exact hour

The numbers agree with the feminine noun ώρα (hour): use μία, not ένα, for one o’clock.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Time</th><th scope="col">Greek</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι μία.</td></tr>
<tr><td>2:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι δύο.</td></tr>
<tr><td>3:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι τρεις.</td></tr>
<tr><td>4:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι τέσσερις.</td></tr>
<tr><td>5:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι πέντε.</td></tr>
<tr><td>6:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι έξι.</td></tr>
<tr><td>7:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι επτά / εφτά.</td></tr>
<tr><td>8:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι οκτώ / οχτώ.</td></tr>
<tr><td>9:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι εννέα / εννιά.</td></tr>
<tr><td>10:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι δέκα.</td></tr>
<tr><td>11:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι έντεκα.</td></tr>
<tr><td>12:00</td><td lang="el">Είναι δώδεκα.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

Both forms of seven, eight, and nine are correct; εφτά, οχτώ, and εννιά are common in everyday speech.

You can add η ώρα (Είναι τρεις η ώρα), but it is usually omitted in conversation.

## 3. Minutes past the hour — και

Use και (“and”) to add minutes after the hour.

**Hour + και + minutes**

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Time</th><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>3:05</td><td lang="el">Τρεις και πέντε</td><td>Five past three</td></tr>
<tr><td>3:10</td><td lang="el">Τρεις και δέκα</td><td>Ten past three</td></tr>
<tr><td>3:20</td><td lang="el">Τρεις και είκοσι</td><td>Twenty past three</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

You can answer with or without Είναι: Είναι τρεις και δέκα or Τρεις και δέκα.

## 4. Quarter past — και τέταρτο

Τέταρτο means a quarter of an hour (15 minutes).

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Time</th><th scope="col">Greek</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>1:15</td><td lang="el">Μία και τέταρτο</td></tr>
<tr><td>4:15</td><td lang="el">Τέσσερις και τέταρτο</td></tr>
<tr><td>7:15</td><td lang="el">Εφτά και τέταρτο</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

You can also use the number of minutes: τρεις και δεκαπέντε (3:15).

## 5. Half past — και μισή

Use και μισή for half past. Μισή is feminine because it refers to μισή ώρα (half an hour).

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Time</th><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>2:30</td><td lang="el">Δύο και μισή</td><td>Half past two</td></tr>
<tr><td>3:30</td><td lang="el">Τρεις και μισή</td><td>Half past three</td></tr>
<tr><td>6:30</td><td lang="el">Έξι και μισή</td><td>Half past six</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

You can also say τρεις και τριάντα (three thirty).

## 6. Minutes to the next hour — παρά

Παρά means “minus” here. Count back from the **next** hour.

**Next hour + παρά + minutes remaining**

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Time</th><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>3:40</td><td lang="el">Τέσσερις παρά είκοσι</td><td>Twenty to four</td></tr>
<tr><td>3:50</td><td lang="el">Τέσσερις παρά δέκα</td><td>Ten to four</td></tr>
<tr><td>3:55</td><td lang="el">Τέσσερις παρά πέντε</td><td>Five to four</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

You can also read the hour and minutes directly: τρεις και σαράντα (3:40).

## 7. Quarter to — παρά τέταρτο

Use παρά τέταρτο with the next hour.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Time</th><th scope="col">Greek</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>2:45</td><td lang="el">Τρεις παρά τέταρτο</td></tr>
<tr><td>8:45</td><td lang="el">Εννιά παρά τέταρτο</td></tr>
<tr><td>12:45</td><td lang="el">Μία παρά τέταρτο</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

For 12:45, the next hour is one: μία παρά τέταρτο.

## 8. Parts of the day

To distinguish morning from evening, add a phrase such as το πρωί or το βράδυ.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">το πρωί</td><td>in the morning</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το μεσημέρι</td><td>at midday / in the early afternoon</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το απόγευμα</td><td>in the afternoon</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το βράδυ</td><td>in the evening / at night</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">τη νύχτα</td><td>at night</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">τα μεσάνυχτα</td><td>at midnight</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι οκτώ <strong>το πρωί</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s eight in the morning.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι εννιά <strong>το βράδυ</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s nine in the evening.</p>
</div>

The boundaries are flexible: το μεσημέρι can include early afternoon, while το απόγευμα refers to later afternoon.

For noon and midnight, say Είναι μεσημέρι (It’s noon) and Είναι μεσάνυχτα (It’s midnight).

## 9. Reading the 24-hour clock

Written schedules often use the 24-hour clock. You can read the numbers directly or convert them to a 12-hour expression.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Written time</th><th scope="col">24-hour reading</th><th scope="col">12-hour expression</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>14:00</td><td lang="el">Δεκατέσσερις</td><td lang="el">Δύο το μεσημέρι</td></tr>
<tr><td>18:30</td><td lang="el">Δεκαοκτώ και τριάντα</td><td lang="el">Έξι και μισή το απόγευμα</td></tr>
<tr><td>21:00</td><td lang="el">Είκοσι μία</td><td lang="el">Εννιά το βράδυ</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

The 12-hour expressions are more usual in everyday conversation.

## 10. Saying “at” a particular time

Compare telling the time with saying when something happens:

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι τρεις.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s three o’clock.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Θα φύγουμε <strong>στις τρεις</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">We’ll leave at three o’clock.</p>
</div>

Use **στη μία** for “at one” and **στις** for the other hours. These combine σε with the accusative article: σε + τη → στη, σε + τις → στις. One hour takes the singular; the other hours take the plural.

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Θα έρθω <strong>στη μία</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I’ll come at one.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Το μάθημα αρχίζει <strong>στις έξι και μισή</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The lesson starts at half past six.</p>
</div>

## 11. Asking what time something happens

Use Τι ώρα…; to ask when something happens.

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Τι ώρα</strong> θα έρθεις;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">What time will you come?</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Τι ώρα</strong> αρχίζει η ταινία;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">What time does the film start?</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Τι ώρα</strong> κλείνει το μουσείο;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">What time does the museum close?</p>
</div>

Στις πόσες…; is an alternative in conversation:

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Στις πόσες</strong> θα έρθεις;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">What time will you come?</p>
</div>

## 12. Saying “from … until …”

- **από** — from
- **μέχρι** — until

Keep the article before the hour: **τις**, or **τη** for one o’clock.

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Το μαγαζί είναι ανοιχτό <strong>από τις εννιά μέχρι τις πέντε</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The shop is open from nine until five.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Θα είμαι εδώ <strong>από τη μία μέχρι τις τρεις</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I’ll be here from one until three.</p>
</div>

## 14. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek. Write the times in words.

1. What time does the shop close?

2. It’s exactly four o’clock.

3. It’s ten past five.

4. It’s quarter past nine.

5. It’s half past eight in the morning.

6. It’s ten to one.

7. It’s 20:45. (Give both a 24-hour reading and a conversational 12-hour expression, including the part of the day in the latter.)

8. I’ll come around one o’clock.

9. What time will you leave? At half past nine. (To one person informally.)

10. The shop is open from eight until six.
