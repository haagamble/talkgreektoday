## 1. Close family

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">η μητέρα</td><td>mother</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">ο πατέρας</td><td>father</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">ο αδελφός</td><td>brother</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η αδελφή</td><td>sister</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">ο γιος</td><td>son</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η κόρη</td><td>daughter</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

You may also see **αδερφός** and **αδερφή** for brother and sister.

The plural **γιοι** (sons) is irregular.

## 4. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek.

1. The sister is here.
2. Do you see the babies? (One person, informally.)
3. We have four granddaughters and five grandsons.
4. The children drink juice.
5. He sees the grandfather.
6. The grandmother has eight grandchildren.
7. You see the children. (Several people, or one person politely.)
8. I have a sibling.
9. The father is tired.
10. The cousins are here.
