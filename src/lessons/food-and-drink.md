## 1. Food

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">το ψωμί</td><td>bread</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το τυρί</td><td>cheese</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το αυγό</td><td>egg</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το κοτόπουλο</td><td>chicken</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">το ρύζι</td><td>rice</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η σαλάτα</td><td>salad</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η σοκολάτα</td><td>chocolate</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

When talking about an unspecified amount of food, leave out the article: **ψωμί** (bread), **τυρί** (cheese).

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Έχουμε <strong>ψωμί και τυρί</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">We have <strong>bread and cheese</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η Μαρία έχει <strong>αυγά</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Maria has <strong>eggs</strong>.</p>
</div>

## 3. To eat: τρώω

**Τρώω** means “I eat” or “I am eating.”

The endings of **τρώω** are slightly different from the endings of **βλέπω**.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Person</th><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">εγώ</td><td lang="el">τρώω</td><td>I eat</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">εσύ</td><td lang="el">τρως</td><td>you eat (singular)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">αυτός / αυτή / αυτό</td><td lang="el">τρώει</td><td>he / she / it eats</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">εμείς</td><td lang="el">τρώμε</td><td>we eat</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">εσείς</td><td lang="el">τρώτε</td><td>you eat (plural / polite)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">αυτοί / αυτές / αυτά</td><td lang="el">τρώνε</td><td>they eat</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Τρώω</strong> κοτόπουλο και ρύζι.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I’m <strong>eating</strong> chicken and rice.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η Μαρία <strong>τρώει</strong> μια σαλάτα.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Maria is <strong>eating</strong> a salad.</p>
</div>

With masculine food and drink nouns, use these accusative forms:

| Nominative | Accusative |
| --- | --- |
| ο καφές | τον καφέ |
| ένας καφές | έναν καφέ |
| ο χυμός | τον χυμό |
| ένας χυμός | έναν χυμό |


## 5. To drink: πίνω

Here are the present-tense forms of **πίνω** (to drink):

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Person</th><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>εγώ</td><td lang="el">πίνω</td><td>I drink</td></tr>
<tr><td>εσύ</td><td lang="el">πίνεις</td><td>you drink (singular)</td></tr>
<tr><td>αυτός / αυτή / αυτό</td><td lang="el">πίνει</td><td>he / she / it drinks</td></tr>
<tr><td>εμείς</td><td lang="el">πίνουμε</td><td>we drink</td></tr>
<tr><td>εσείς</td><td lang="el">πίνετε</td><td>you drink (plural / polite)</td></tr>
<tr><td>αυτοί / αυτές / αυτά</td><td lang="el">πίνουν(ε)</td><td>they drink</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Πίνω</strong> καφέ.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I <strong>drink</strong> coffee.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Τα παιδιά <strong>πίνουν</strong> γάλα.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">The children are <strong>drinking</strong> milk.</p>
</div>


## 6. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek.

1. We are eating bread and cheese.
2. Do you drink coffee? (Singular.)
3. Maria has tomatoes.
4. I am not drinking milk.
5. The children are eating bananas.
6. We eat chicken and potatoes.
7. Do you eat eggs? (Plural or polite.)
8. She eats an apple and an orange.
9. They have rice and salad.
10. I drink tea and eat chocolate.
