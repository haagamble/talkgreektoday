## 1. Gender

Greek has three genders: masculine, feminine, and neuter. The words for **the** and **a/an** change to match the noun.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Gender</th><th scope="col">The</th><th scope="col">A / an</th><th scope="col">Example</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Masculine</td><td lang="el">ο</td><td lang="el">ένας</td><td lang="el">ο σκύλος / ένας σκύλος</td></tr>
<tr><td>Feminine</td><td lang="el">η</td><td lang="el">μία / μια</td><td lang="el">η καρέκλα / μια καρέκλα</td></tr>
<tr><td>Neuter</td><td lang="el">το</td><td lang="el">ένα</td><td lang="el">το κινητό / ένα κινητό</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

Learn each noun with its article.

## 2. Using the definite article — the

Use **ο, η, το** when talking about a particular person or thing.

- **είναι** — is
- **εδώ** — here
- **εκεί** — there
- **και** — and

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Το σπίτι</strong> είναι εδώ.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>The house</strong> is here.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Το βιβλίο</strong> είναι εκεί.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>The book</strong> is there.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Η γάτα</strong> είναι εδώ και <strong>ο σκύλος</strong> είναι εκεί.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>The cat</strong> is here, and <strong>the dog</strong> is there.</p>
</div>

## 3. Using the indefinite article — a / an

Use **ένας, μία / μια, ένα** when introducing a person or thing that is not specific.

Without a named subject, **Είναι…** can mean “It’s…”.

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι <strong>ένα κινητό</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s <strong>a cell phone</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι <strong>μια καρέκλα</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s <strong>a chair</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι <strong>ένας δρόμος</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s <strong>a road</strong>.</p>
</div>

**Μία** and **μια** are both correct. **Μια** is common in everyday speech.

## 4. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek using the articles introduced above.

1. The house is there.

2. The cell phone is there.

3. It’s a road.

4. The chair is here.

5. It’s a door, and the key is here.

6. The child is here.

7. The lamp is here.

8. A book and a notebook.

9. A pen and a pencil.

10. The cat is here, and the dog is there.
