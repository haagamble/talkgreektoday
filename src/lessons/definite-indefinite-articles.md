## 1. Gender

Greek has three genders: masculine, feminine, and neuter. The words for **the** and **a/an** change to match the noun.

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Gender</th><th scope="col">The</th><th scope="col">A / an</th><th scope="col">Example</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Masculine</td><td lang="el">ο</td><td lang="el">ένας</td><td lang="el">ο φίλος / ένας φίλος</td></tr>
<tr><td>Feminine</td><td lang="el">η</td><td lang="el">μία / μια</td><td lang="el">η φίλη / μια φίλη</td></tr>
<tr><td>Neuter</td><td lang="el">το</td><td lang="el">ένα</td><td lang="el">το βιβλίο / ένα βιβλίο</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

Learn each noun with its article.

## 2. Using the definite article — the

Use **ο, η, το** when talking about a particular person or thing.

- **είναι** — is
- **εδώ** — here

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Ο σκύλος</strong> είναι εδώ.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>The dog</strong> is here.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Η γάτα</strong> είναι εδώ.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>The cat</strong> is here.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el"><strong>Το βιβλίο</strong> είναι εδώ.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>The book</strong> is here.</p>
</div>

Without a named subject, **Είναι…** can mean “It’s…”.

## 3. Using the indefinite article — a / an

Use **ένας, μία / μια, ένα** when introducing a person or thing that is not specific.

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι <strong>ένας σκύλος</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s <strong>a dog</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι <strong>μια γάτα</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s <strong>a cat</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είναι <strong>ένα βιβλίο</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">It’s <strong>a book</strong>.</p>
</div>

**Μία** and **μια** are both correct. **Μια** is common in everyday speech.

## 4. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek using the articles introduced above.

1. The house is here.

2. The male friend is here.

3. It’s a road.

4. The female friend is here.

5. It’s a door.

6. The child is here.

7. The girl is here.

8. A book and a house.

9. A cat and a dog.

10. The book and the cat.
