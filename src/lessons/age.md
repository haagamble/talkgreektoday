## 1. Saying your age

Use **είμαι + number + χρονών** to say how old someone is. Learn **χρονών** as part of the expression “years old.”

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Είμαι <strong>είκοσι πέντε χρονών</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I’m <strong>twenty-five years old</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Ο πατέρας μου είναι <strong>εξήντα χρονών</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">My father is <strong>sixty years old</strong>.</p>
</div>

Some numbers use genitive forms in this expression:

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Age</th><th scope="col">Greek</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>21 years old</td><td lang="el">είκοσι <strong>ενός</strong> χρονών</td></tr>
<tr><td>23 years old</td><td lang="el">είκοσι <strong>τριών</strong> χρονών</td></tr>
<tr><td>24 years old</td><td lang="el">είκοσι <strong>τεσσάρων</strong> χρονών</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

The same forms apply after other tens: **τριάντα ενός**, **σαράντα τριών**, **πενήντα τεσσάρων**. For ages 3 and 4 use **τριών χρονών**, **τεσσάρων χρονών**; for 13 and 14 use **δεκατριών χρονών**, **δεκατεσσάρων χρονών**. For a one-year-old, use **ενός χρόνου**.

## 2. Asking someone’s age

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">Πόσων χρονών είσαι;</td><td>How old are you? (Singular, informal.)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Πόσων χρονών είστε;</td><td>How old are you? (Plural or polite.)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Πόσων χρονών είναι η κόρη σου;</td><td>How old is your daughter?</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

Learn **Πόσων χρονών…;** as the expression “How old…?”

## 3. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek. Write numbers in words.

1. I’m twenty-five years old.
2. My father is sixty years old.
3. How old are you? (Singular, informal.)
4. Maria is thirty-two years old.
5. My brother is twenty-one years old.
6. How old is your son?
7. My mother is fifty-six years old.
8. Are you forty years old? (Singular, informal.)
9. My sister is twenty-four years old.
10. How old is your daughter?
