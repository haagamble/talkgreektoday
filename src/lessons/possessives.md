## 1. Possessive words

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el"><strong>μου</strong></td><td>my</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el"><strong>σου</strong></td><td>your (singular, informal)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el"><strong>του</strong></td><td>his / its</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el"><strong>της</strong></td><td>her</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el"><strong>μας</strong></td><td>our</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el"><strong>σας</strong></td><td>your (plural or polite)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el"><strong>τους</strong></td><td>their</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

Put the possessive word after the noun. Keep the definite article before it:

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">η μητέρα <strong>μου</strong></td><td><strong>my</strong> mother</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">ο αδελφός <strong>σου</strong></td><td><strong>your</strong> brother</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">ο γιος <strong>του</strong></td><td><strong>his</strong> son</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">η κόρη <strong>της</strong></td><td><strong>her</strong> daughter</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">τα παιδιά <strong>μας</strong></td><td><strong>our</strong> children</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">οι γονείς <strong>σας</strong></td><td><strong>your</strong> parents</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">οι αδελφές <strong>τους</strong></td><td><strong>their</strong> sisters</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

These possessive words stay the same with masculine, feminine, and neuter nouns, and with plurals.

## 2. Using possessives in sentences

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Ο γιος μου έχει ένα σκύλο.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>My son</strong> has a dog.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η κόρη σου δεν είναι εδώ.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>Your daughter</strong> is not here.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Έχεις τα κλειδιά σου;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Do you have <strong>your keys</strong>?</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Έχω το βιβλίο μου.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I have <strong>my book</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η μητέρα του πίνει καφέ.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>His mother</strong> drinks coffee.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Ο πατέρας της βλέπει τον δρόμο.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>Her father</strong> sees the road.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Τα μήλα μας δεν είναι πράσινα.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>Our apples</strong> are not green.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Το σπίτι τους είναι εκεί.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en"><strong>Their house</strong> is there.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Έχεις το κινητό μου;</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Do you have <strong>my cell phone</strong>?</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Βλέπω τη μητέρα μου.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I see <strong>my mother</strong>.</p>
</div>

## 3. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek.

1. My mother is here.
2. His father is drinking water.
3. I am eating my apple.
4. Is her brother here?
5. Our mother has two daughters.
6. Their aunt has a baby.
7. Her father drinks coffee.
8. You have your books. (Plural or polite.)
9. I see his three sons.
10. Our children are not drinking milk.
