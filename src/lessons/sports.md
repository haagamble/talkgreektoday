## 1. Sports and activities

- **το ποδόσφαιρο** — football / soccer
- **το μπάσκετ** — basketball
- **το τένις** — tennis
- **το κολύμπι** — swimming
- **το τρέξιμο** — running
- **η γυμναστική** — exercise / working out
- **η ομάδα** — team
- **ο αγώνας** — match / game
- **ο παίκτης** — player
- **το γήπεδο** — sports ground / court / stadium

## 2. Useful verbs

- **παίζω** — I play
- **τρέχω** — I run
- **κολυμπάω** — I swim
- **βλέπω** — I watch

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Βλέπω <strong>τον αγώνα</strong>.</p>
<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I’m watching the match.</p>
</div>

<div class="example">
<p class="greek-line" lang="el">Η ομάδα παίζει <strong>στο γήπεδο</strong>.</p>

</div>

<div class="example">

<p class="translation-line" lang="en">Who is the player?</p>

<p class="translation-line" lang="en">I’m not watching the match.</p>

## 5. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek.

1. I play football.
2. She plays tennis.
3. I run every day.
4. The children swim.
5. We watch the match.
6. The team plays at the stadium.
7. He plays basketball.
8. I do exercise.
9. Who is the player?
10. I’m not watching the match.
