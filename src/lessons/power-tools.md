Power tools are phrases and sentences that help you learn and communicate in Greek. Do not worry about understanding all the grammar yet. Try to memorize these phrases so you can use them, and add others as you need them.

## 1. Talking about your learning

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">Μαθαίνω ελληνικά.</td><td>I’m learning Greek.</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Μιλάω λίγο ελληνικά.</td><td>I speak a little Greek.</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Θέλω να εξασκηθώ.</td><td>I want to practise.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

## 2. When you do not understand

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">Δεν καταλαβαίνω.</td><td>I don’t understand.</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Δεν καταλαβαίνω καλά.</td><td>I don’t understand well.</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Μιλάτε αγγλικά;</td><td>Do you speak English? (Polite or plural.)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Συγγνώμη.</td><td>Sorry / Excuse me.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
