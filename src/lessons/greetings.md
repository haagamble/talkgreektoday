## 1. Saying hello

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th><th scope="col">When to use it</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">Γεια σου!</td><td>Hello! / Hi!</td><td>To one person informally</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Γεια σας!</td><td>Hello!</td><td>To several people, or one person politely</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Καλημέρα!</td><td>Good morning!</td><td>In the morning</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Καλησπέρα!</td><td>Good afternoon! / Good evening!</td><td>In the afternoon or evening</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

Learn **Γεια σου** and **Γεια σας** as whole expressions. They can also mean “bye.”

## 2. Asking how someone is

- **πώς** — how
- **ευχαριστώ** — thank you
- **πολύ** — very
- **καλά** — well / fine

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">Πώς είσαι;</td><td>How are you? (One person, informal.)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Πώς είστε;</td><td>How are you? (Several people, or one person politely.)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Είμαι καλά.</td><td>I’m fine.</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Καλά, ευχαριστώ.</td><td>Fine, thank you.</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Πολύ καλά, ευχαριστώ.</td><td>Very well, thank you.</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Εσύ;</td><td>And you? (One person, informal.)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Εσείς;</td><td>And you? (Several people, or one person politely.)</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

- **Ναι** — yes
- **Όχι** — no

## 4. Saying goodbye

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th scope="col">Greek</th><th scope="col">English</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td lang="el">Αντίο!</td><td>Goodbye!</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Καλό βράδυ!</td><td>Have a good evening!</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="el">Καληνύχτα!</td><td>Good night!</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>

Use **Καλησπέρα** when greeting someone. Use **Καλό βράδυ** when leaving, and **Καληνύχτα** when saying goodbye late at night or before bed.

## 5. Practice exercises

Translate into Greek. Address one person informally unless otherwise indicated.

1. Good morning! How are you?

2. Good afternoon! How are you? (To one person politely.)

3. Fine, thank you. And you?

4. Very well, thank you. And you? (To several people.)

5. Hello! Are you ready? (To one woman.)

6. Good evening! I’m fine, thank you.

7. Goodbye! Have a good evening!

8. Goodbye! Good night!

9. Good morning! We are here.

10. Hello! How are you? (To several people.)
